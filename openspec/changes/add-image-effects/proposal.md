# Add image effects

**Roadmap phase:** Phase 2 (milestone 0.2–0.4)

## Why
Borders, edges and shadows make screenshots fit polished documentation; capture info adds traceability.

## What Changes
- Effects panel: border, edges, shadow, perspective, page curl, reflection, colour adjustment, filters, watermark, capture info
- Effects apply to the whole image or a selection and stay editable
- Quick effects (saved effect combinations)

## Benchmark features covered
- Border
- Edges (torn, wave, zigzag, fade)
- Shadow
- Perspective
- Page curl
- Reflection
- Colour adjustment (brightness, contrast, hue, saturation, gamma)
- Filters (grayscale, sepia, invert, sharpen, soften)
- Colour replacement
- Watermark
- Capture info (date, app, OS, custom text)

## Out of scope
- Animated backgrounds

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `image-effects`

### Modified Capabilities
- none

## Impact
- Depends on the MVP changes being archived first.
