# Add advanced annotation tools

**Roadmap phase:** Phase 2 (milestone 0.2–0.4)

## Why
Beyond arrows and boxes, good documentation screenshots zoom into details, dim distractions and remove clutter.

## What Changes
- Magnifier with offset lens
- Spotlight (dim and/or blur outside a shape)
- Stamps with original Annota stamp sets and custom stamps
- Cut-out (remove a horizontal/vertical strip and join)
- Selection tool with move/delete and auto-fill
- Fill (flood fill) with tolerance
- Eraser
- Magic wand selection

## Benchmark features covered
- Magnify
- Spotlight
- Stamps incl. custom stamps
- Cut out with edge styles
- Selection (rect/ellipse/polygon/lasso) with auto-fill
- Fill with tolerance and global fill
- Eraser
- Magic wand
- Text-only highlight/blur

## Out of scope
- Smart Move / Simplify (Phase 3)
