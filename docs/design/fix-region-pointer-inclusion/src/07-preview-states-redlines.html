1 · Preview arrow

Drawn at the pointer hot spot while Include Pointer is on. Same outline as the arrow frame::pointer_path composites into the image: 22 px tall, 15 px wide, hot spot at the tip, body extending right and down. Drawn under the system cursor, never over the toolbar or the hint bar.

In captureWhite fill, 1 px #000006 outline on a 2.8 px #000006 halo. Hot spot inside the selection.
Not in capture48 % white fill, 1.5 px white outline on a 3 px rgb(0 0 6 / 60 %) halo, 2.1 px white slash on a 4.4 px dark halo. Hot spot outside, or exactly on, the selection border.

2 · Pointer chip

Anchored 30 px right of the hot spot, vertically centred on it; flips to 30 px left of the hot spot when it would leave the monitor. Same body as the loupe chip (--osd-bg-solid, radius 6, padding 4 / 10 / 4 / 12, 13 px bold) plus a 3 px inset keyline on the leading edge.
